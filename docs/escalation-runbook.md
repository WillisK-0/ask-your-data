# Escalation runbook

## When to escalate
Escalate to Tier 2 when a ticket is a confirmed bug, involves data loss, or has breached its first-response SLA. Escalate to the Escalations team for any urgent ticket older than 4 hours without a resolution or any ticket from an Enterprise customer with CSAT below 3.

## How to escalate
Set status to escalated, assign to an Escalations agent, and post the ticket link in the escalations channel with the customer's plan and the SLA breach time. Do not promise timelines to the customer before Tier 2 has triaged.

## Churn risk
A customer with two or more overdue invoices and an open urgent ticket is flagged as churn risk. Account management is notified the same day.
