# Refund policy

## Eligibility
Refunds are available within 14 days of the first charge on a new subscription. Renewals are not refundable, but unused seats removed mid-cycle are credited to the next invoice at the daily prorated rate.

## Duplicate charges
A duplicate charge is refunded in full within 5 business days once Billing confirms it. Agents on Tier 1 may approve duplicate-charge refunds up to $600 without escalation.

## Enterprise plans
Enterprise refunds require account-manager approval and a signed change order. Standard refund windows do not apply.
