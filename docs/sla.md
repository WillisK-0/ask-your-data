# Support service levels

## First response targets
- Urgent: 1 hour, 24x7
- High: 4 business hours
- Normal: 1 business day
- Low: 2 business days

## Resolution targets
Bugs confirmed as product defects target resolution within 5 business days. Workarounds count as resolution if the customer accepts them in writing.

## Business and Enterprise plans
Business and Enterprise customers get a named escalation contact and a 30-minute urgent first-response target during US business hours.
